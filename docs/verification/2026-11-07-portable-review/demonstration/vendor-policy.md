# Example supplier AI governance policy

Synthetic demonstration document. This is an invented supplier policy for learning and testing; it is not evidence about a real organisation and is not a recommended policy template.

The supplier develops an AI assistant that drafts responses to requests for public services. The assistant provides recommendations to staff; it must not make final eligibility decisions or issue notices without human approval.

The supplier is committed to transparency, accountability and responsible AI. The service manager must maintain a record of material incidents and arrange an annual review of the service. Staff may raise concerns with the service manager. The supplier will monitor the system and consider improvements when appropriate.

The procurement team must request evidence of testing before deployment. The supplier must describe the intended use, material limitations and known risks. A human reviewer must check a draft before it is sent to a member of the public. The service manager may suspend the assistant following a serious incident. The organisation will retain appropriate records.

The supplier aims to explain decisions and support affected people. The supplier will determine implementation arrangements during service delivery.
