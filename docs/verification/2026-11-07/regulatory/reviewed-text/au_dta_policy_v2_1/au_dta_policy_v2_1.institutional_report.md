## Hidden failure pathways

Failure pathway summaries below show how paperwork compliance can proceed without live operational control.

### PATH-001 — Obligations are imposed with no precedence rule between them — failure pathway

- The document raises **mandatory language (shall/must)** at “Page iii”: “requirements agencies must follow. Recommended actions and considerations are also included where relevant.”
- The document imposes duties but never says which provision prevails if two conflict, which cannot be waived, or what the document is subordinate to. A routine revision can therefore change what it actually requires without anyone treating that as a change of standard, and a reader facing two provisions that point different ways has no rule for deciding which governs.
- The institution cites this expectation as assurance, but the closing control — a precedence clause stating which provisions prevail on conflict, which may not be waived or amended except by a named authority, and what instrument this document is subordinate to — does not exist or is not current.
- A decision or deployment proceeds on the strength of the document alone.
- If the outcome is wrong, there is no record that this expectation controlled the decision, and no defined route to correct it.
- **Escalation gate:** No amendment is made without recording whether it changes a provision the precedence clause protects.

### PATH-002 — Government AI policy requires exception and incident escalation consequence — failure pathway

- The document raises **mandatory language (shall/must)** at “Page iii”: “requirements agencies must follow. Recommended actions and considerations are also included where relevant.”
- The document states requirements but attaches no consequence to breaching them, so compliance rests on goodwill and a breach produces no defined institutional response.
- The institution cites this expectation as assurance, but the closing control — consequence schedule mapping each class of breach to a defined response, up to suspension of use — does not exist or is not current.
- A decision or deployment proceeds on the strength of the document alone.
- If the outcome is wrong, there is no record that this expectation controlled the decision, and no defined route to correct it.
- **Escalation gate:** No breach is closed without a recorded consequence or a documented, authorised exception.

### PATH-003 — Risk is discussed without a gate that can stop deployment — failure pathway
