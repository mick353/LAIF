## Hidden failure pathways

Failure pathway summaries below show how paperwork compliance can proceed without live operational control.

### PATH-001 — Obligations are imposed with no precedence rule between them — failure pathway

- The document raises **mandatory language (shall/must)** at “start of document”: “requirements agencies must follow. Recommended actions and considerations are also included where relevant.”
- The document imposes duties but never says which provision prevails if two conflict, which cannot be waived, or what the document is subordinate to. A routine revision can therefore change what it actually requires without anyone treating that as a change of standard, and a reader facing two provisions that point different ways has no rule for deciding which governs.
- The institution cites this expectation as assurance, but the closing control — a precedence clause stating which provisions prevail on conflict, which may not be waived or amended except by a named authority, and what instrument this document is subordinate to — does not exist or is not current.
- A decision or deployment proceeds on the strength of the document alone.
- If the outcome is wrong, there is no record that this expectation controlled the decision, and no defined route to correct it.
- **Escalation gate:** No amendment is made without recording whether it changes a provision the precedence clause protects.

### PATH-002 — Risk is discussed without a gate that can stop deployment — failure pathway

- The document raises **risk governance** at “start of document”: “and undertake risk-based use case-level actions. In recognition of the speed and scale of change in this area,”
- The document engages with risk but sets no precondition that must hold before deployment proceeds, so risk can be documented and deployment can continue regardless of what the assessment found.
- The institution cites this expectation as assurance, but the closing control — deployment gate listing the conditions that must all hold before go-live, and who signs each — does not exist or is not current.
- A decision or deployment proceeds on the strength of the document alone.
- If the outcome is wrong, there is no record that this expectation controlled the decision, and no defined route to correct it.
- **Escalation gate:** Deployment does not proceed while any gate condition is unmet.

### PATH-003 — Affected people have no route to challenge an outcome — failure pathway

- The document raises **safety** at “start of document”: “a pathway for staff to report AI safety concerns, including AI incidents. • pathways for the public to report AI safety concerns,”
- The document addresses safety but gives the people affected by a decision nothing they can invoke — no appeal, review, or remedy — so harm to an individual has no defined path to correction.
- The institution cites this expectation as assurance, but the closing control — published route by which an affected person can contest an outcome, with owner, timescale, and reversal authority — does not exist or is not current.
- A decision or deployment proceeds on the strength of the document alone.
- If the outcome is wrong, there is no record that this expectation controlled the decision, and no defined route to correct it.
- **Escalation gate:** No decision pathway operates without a stated contest route.
