# Review of the substantive LAIF reasoning

Reviewed 7 November 2026 against the committed principal text and exact DTA/EU source evidence. This is a critical self-review, not an independently commissioned validation. It concerns the logic of the authored assessments; tests do not establish that their judgements are correct.

| Claim reviewed | Governing basis and evidence | Review finding and correction |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete source mapping establishes instrument failure | Part Five requires three satisfactory answers; §7.1 concerns conduct inconsistent with a requirement and producing/likely to produce relevant harm. | Unsupported conversion of uncertainty into an adverse result. Both overall determinations now state **not demonstrated**, with no global structural failure established. A specific contradictory provision could justify a different finding, but none is established by merely missing part of the governing corpus. |
| Missing system results count against a legislative instrument | §§2.1–2.5 concern system properties; §§9.1/9.4 concern required deployment evidence. | The proper instrument question is what verification the rules require. Actual behaviour is a separate assessment subject. The reports now distinguish these questions and do not require legislation to contain individual test results. |
| §9.1 automatically creates a presumption against the regulator | §9.1 is a PDCA/deployment gate; §7.1 burdens have their stated proceeding/conduct context. | No assessed deployment, omitted mandatory PDCA or failure to produce required evidence was established. No such presumption is applied. |
| DTA screening/treatment equals full structural Coupling | Q004/Q007/Q008 and Part One Principle 2 / Part Five Q1. | Direct risk-to-treatment linkage is demonstrated. Inseparability and the three normative-force limbs are not fully mapped. Q1a no longer labels that narrower evidence a complete explicit structural pairing. |
| EU legal bindingness establishes full Q1b HARD equivalence | Original Articles 85/86; amended safeguards; §2.4/Part Five require equivalent scope, access and enforcement. | Statutory duties and rights are demonstrated; full equivalent force across the package is not. The package-level HARD classification is withdrawn, while actual rights and positive clause-level pairings remain credited. |
| Transition dates prove an unprotected interval | Amendment p35 Q020–Q023 establishes dates; original explanation/complaint rights and other law remain relevant. | Timing changes are verified. Absence of adequate interim protection is not established. The report identifies a mapping dependency, not proven substantive incoherence of postponement. |
| Irreversible effects necessarily fail Q3 | Part Five Q3 permits irreversibility with proportionate justification and strongest appropriate procedure; D1 requires scaled authorisation. | No universal guarantee of undoing every consequence is imposed. Proposed controls are possible closing evidence, not newly invented mandatory remedies. |
| All four Integrity Layer properties should be considered | §2.5 and §§9.1/9.4 expressly include calibration; §2.4 retains three-property wording. | Including calibration is a defensible disclosed working reading; its applicability condition matters. The inconsistency is a genuine framework defect requiring a controlled correction, not silent reinterpretation. |
| Positive source findings survive the review | DTA mandatory assessment/change/oversight controls; EU conditional protection floors, explanation, complaint, override/stop and review provisions. | Retained. These are actual correspondences, neither erased by incomplete package-level mapping nor sufficient by themselves to certify every system. |

The resulting assessments remain substantive: they identify interests, permissions, paired protections, possible asymmetries, scale counterexamples, irreversible consequences, evidence and closing actions. Their overall conclusions are now limited to what that reasoning establishes. The remaining engine/framework defects warrant implementation work; they are not fixed by editorial qualifications alone.
