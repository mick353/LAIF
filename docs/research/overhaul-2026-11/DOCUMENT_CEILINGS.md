# What a document can establish

Exploratory interpretation note; preserves the existing distinction in
[Substantive review](../../governance/SUBSTANTIVE_REVIEW.md). This is not a new
scoring rule or a canonical amendment. Site presentation remains pending.

The existing authored label is **SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE**, not unqualified
"established". Its scope matters. A document can establish that a specified
requirement is written into that instrument. It cannot by itself demonstrate
that an implemented system follows the requirement. Assigning an unconditional
"partial support" ceiling would incorrectly prevent recognition of complete
documentary design; assigning an unconditional "established" ceiling would
overstate operational evidence.

For all eight criteria the highest available *documentary* determination is
SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE when the author supplies exact evidence and a sufficient,
explicitly limited argument. This is permitted by the current schema, not a
promise that any selected document deserves it. For a claim about actual
implementation, policy text alone leaves that claim NOT_ESTABLISHED; partial
documentary support can be reported separately. Partial support is not an
automatic minimum or default.

| Criterion | What can receive documentary support | Evidence still needed for an operational conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| A.1 | Required explanation of outputs, uncertainty and limitations for affected people | Actual explanations, access tests and evidence that users can obtain and understand them |
| A.2 | Stated requirements tying objectives to implementation and behaviour across testing/deployment | Objective/implementation comparison and appropriately scoped behavioural evaluations |
| A.3 | Defined limits, intervention, escalation and authorisation requirements | Tests and operating records showing boundaries and intervention work under relevant conditions |
| A.4 | Applicable requirement to compare expressed confidence with observed accuracy | Sourced measurements, population, uncertainty and relevant empirical calibration evidence; applicability itself needs an explicit argument |
| Q1 | Specified restriction/permission paired with a protected interest and sufficiently reasoned equivalent force | Authority/applicability analysis and evidence the paired protection can actually be invoked and enforced |
| Q2 | Explicit smallest-actor and general-application reasoning with justified exceptions | Context-specific review of comparable actors and consequences; textual consistency alone cannot settle all applications |
| Q3 | Revision/decommissioning procedures and proportionate authority for irreversible decisions | Exercised rollback/revision evidence and analysis of irreversible effects that rollback cannot undo |
| G.1 | Governance actors themselves subject to transparency, review and effective challenge | Actual review/access/challenge records and evidence of effective responses |

A document containing an empirical study or authenticated operating records is
not merely a policy promise. Its evidential contribution must be assessed on
provenance and methods; the file format does not set the evidence ceiling.
Likewise, an exact legal quotation does not settle current applicability,
exceptions or enforceability. Native certification remains a separate channel.

Suggested future Site copy beside a result: "This result concerns what the
document requires. It does not establish that the system does it." The card
must also show the actual scope, missing evidence and author attribution.
