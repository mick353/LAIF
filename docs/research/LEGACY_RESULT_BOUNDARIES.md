# Interpretation limits of the historical LAIF comparison reports

**Status:** source-critical commentary, 7 November 2026. The committed
historical reports and scoring engine have **not** been rewritten or silently
recalibrated. This note governs their use in external scholarly work.

The legacy corpus includes **four hash-pinned official excerpts** and **six
illustrative/representative excerpts**. The full report distinguishes those
source tiers. Its aggregate statistics across all ten are not estimates of
international regulatory quality, coverage, control effectiveness or AI
safety. Representative excerpts are not observations of the named official
source and cannot establish its deficiencies.

Some prominently displayed differences reflect LAIF's **own vocabulary**.
For instance, the reported absence of LAIF-native terminology from official
external frameworks does not establish a legal defect, missing protection
or relative inferiority. A structural preference for explicit precedence
rules likewise cannot, by itself, prove that binding-law hierarchies,
conflict rules or enforcement mechanisms are absent elsewhere.

The output also includes the former **US Executive Order 14110**. It was
revoked on 20 January 2025 by Executive Order 14148; an official [US
government record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CDOC-119hdoc28/html/CDOC-119hdoc28.htm)
confirms the revocation. Treat the earlier order as **historical research
material**, not live US AI policy. A cited historical provision could still
matter to a defined historical question, but its legal status must be stated.

Before reusing an earlier comparative finding:

1. Retrieve the corresponding underlying source and exact passage.
2. Determine whether it is an official excerpt or an illustrative surrogate.
3. Independently check currentness, scope, jurisdiction and the broader
   instrument; do not equate excerpts with full legal systems.
4. Replace LAIF-native lexical scores with specific, observable research
   questions and external benchmarks where appropriate.
5. Search both positive counterexamples and contrary evidence; do not infer
   nonexistence from a detector's silence.
6. Report the analysis as unvalidated unless an independent method and
   reviewers have actually established its reliability.

The new [scholarly reporting workflow](SCHOLARLY_REPORTING.md) is the
recommended starting point for external audiences. Its source-quote
inventory is not a retroactive validation of historical LAIF rankings.

**Maintainer follow-up:** any proposed recalibration or alteration of legacy
generated artefacts belongs in a **separate semantic-review PR** with
regenerated reproducibility records. This note authorises no such edits.
