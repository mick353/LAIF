# Research report template: source-first AI governance and safety review

Use this document for a **human-authored scholarly synthesis** after the
[neutral evidence inventory and review protocol](SCHOLARLY_REPORTING.md).
Replace square-bracketed prompts with verified content; delete sections
that truly do not apply. Do not publish bracketed placeholders as findings.
The template grants no source-authentication, legal-opinion or peer-review status.

### 6. AI safety analysis (only if applicable)

[Separate threat hypotheses from demonstrated capabilities and incidents.
Identify system version, access boundaries, deployment context, evaluated

independent tests or live operations. Do not derive future loss-of-control
probabilities from textual scores.]

### 7. Results, non-results and limitations

[Present both strengths and genuine gaps, with numerator/denominator only

### 9. Conclusions and bounded recommendations

[Answer the original question no more strongly than the data allow.
